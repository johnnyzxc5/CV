% --- RESEARCH EXPERIENCE ---
\section*{Research Experience}

\entrytitle{Spectral-Hamiltonian Algebraic Power Evaluation (SHAPE Framework)}{Research Project | KFUPM}\\
Conceived and formulated a physics-guided fault-diagnosis framework combining port-Hamiltonian power structure with differential flatness to construct observer-free residuals. Led the physical simulation and data-generation pipeline, co-designed the multi-joint CNN, and worked directly on its Python/PyTorch implementation using temporal, CWT, and spectral representations.

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Hierarchical Energy-Aware Architecture for Safe Robotic Interaction}{M.Sc. Thesis | KFUPM}\\
Developing a fault-tolerant control architecture for redundant manipulators that combines port-Hamiltonian modelling and energy tanks with geometric trajectory replanning. The work investigates energy freezing and regulated tank exchange while adapting motion on the configuration manifold as actuator failures progressively restrict the robot's reachable configurations.

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Active Fault-Tolerant Flatness-Based Model Predictive Control}{Research Manuscript | Revised for Resubmission}\\
Developed an active fault-tolerant MPC formulation that maps actuator degradation into dynamically consistent constraints in flat-output space. The method adapts feasible trajectory tracking as available control authority decreases, using differential flatness to translate physical actuator limits into algebraic constraints suitable for predictive optimization.

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Geometric Modelling \& Invariant State Estimation of an Underwater Glider}{Coursework \& Research Projects | KFUPM}\\
Modelled an underactuated underwater glider that achieves low-energy locomotion without conventional propulsive actuation, using internal mass displacement and variable buoyancy to modify its centre of gravity and effective density. Studied its nonlinear geometric dynamics and invariant state estimation in MATLAB/Simulink.

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Self-Diagnosing Process Automation \& Predictive Maintenance}{Graduate Research Project | ESPOL}\\
Developed an automated liquid-processing system integrating recipe-based mixing, pumps, motors, pneumatic actuation, PLC/HMI control, and industrial communication through IO-Link. Integrated multi-parameter condition-monitoring sensors and explored MATLAB-based predictive-maintenance and machine-learning workflows for automated fault diagnosis and equipment health assessment.

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Design, Modelling \& Automation of an Industrial Liquid-Filling Machine}{B.Sc. Thesis | ESPOL}\\
Designed and modelled an automated filling machine for viscous liquid products, integrating pneumatic actuation, fluid-flow calculations, electrical sizing, PLC/HMI control, and mechanical design. Performed coupled pneumatic/fluid calculations for dosing accuracy and cycle timing, and used CAD/CAE and finite-element analysis to dimension the liquid hopper and structural components.
