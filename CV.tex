\section*{Research Experience}

\entrytitle{Spectral-Hamiltonian Algebraic Power Evaluation (SHAPE Framework)}{Research Project | KFUPM}\\
Conceived and formulated an observer-free, physics-guided fault detection and isolation methodology interfacing port-Hamiltonian energy balance ($\dot{H} \le y^\top u$) with differential flatness. Formulated the algebraic port-power residual that computes nominal power trajectories directly from reference profiles, eliminating online observer synthesis and numerical differentiation. Led the multi-body physical simulation and trajectory data-generation pipeline on a 6-DOF Kinova Gen3 manipulator. Transitioned the methodology to native numerical tensors (multichannel temporal, CWT time-frequency scalograms, and FFT spectral tensors), co-designed a shared-encoder multi-joint CNN with late fusion, and personally implemented the training, evaluation, and benchmarking pipeline in Python/PyTorch, achieving a 91.7\% macro F1-score across nominal and degraded operating regimes.

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Hierarchical Energy-Aware Architecture for Safe Robotic Interaction}{M.Sc. Thesis | KFUPM}\\
Developing a hierarchical control framework bridging deterministic physical passivity with active fault tolerance in robotic manipulators. Formulated a low-level Port-Hamiltonian Energy Tank supervisor to enforce strict passivity ($\dot{H} \le 0$) and bound energy injection under actuator Loss of Effectiveness (LoE). Developed a coordinate-free high-level trajectory planner parametrizing end-effector pose directly on the Lie group $\mathrm{SE}(3)$ along with kinematic self-motion for 7-DOF redundant manipulators, ensuring dynamic feasibility without coordinate singularities.

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Active Fault-Tolerant Flatness-Based Model Predictive Control}{Research Manuscript | Under Review}\\
Developed an active fault-tolerant model predictive control architecture for nonlinear robotic manipulators subject to progressive actuator degradation. The formulation algebraically maps shrinking physical actuator torque limits into state-dependent acceleration constraints in flat output space, eliminating numerical chattering, reducing energy consumption by 66\% (from 17,064 to 5,668~$\text{N}^2\text{m}^2\text{s}$), and accelerating trajectory optimization to real-time execution speeds (0.01~s).

\vspace{2pt}
\entrytitle{Geometric Modelling and Invariant State Estimation}{Coursework \& Research Projects | KFUPM}\\
Investigated geometric mechanics and symmetry-preserving estimation for underactuated nonlinear dynamical systems. Developed multi-body dynamic models for an underactuated underwater vehicle actuated by internal shifting mass and variable-buoyancy piston systems. Implemented closed-loop simulation and Kalman filtering state estimation architectures in MATLAB, Simulink, and Simscape Multibody, analyzing state reconstruction accuracy under measurement noise and unmodeled hydrodynamics as a foundation for invariant and structure-preserving learning.
